Project Architecture
A comprehensive approach integrating computer vision, spatial localization, 
hybrid deep learning, and explainable AI.

===== PAGE 4 =====
1. Baseline Detection
Establishing robust classification models 
(ResNet/EfficientNet) designed specifically to identify early-
stage infections and subtle disease symptoms from 
standard benchmark datasets.
2. Spatial Localization
Deploying advanced segmentation networks (U-
Net/YOLOv8) to precisely isolate infected leaf regions from 
complex environmental backgrounds, enabling accurate 
severity estimation.
3. Edge-Optimized Hybrid AI
Fusing local feature extraction (MobileNetV3) with global 
contextual reasoning (Vision Transformers) to create highly 
accurate, computationally efficient models for real-time 
deployment.
4. Decision Support & XAI
Integrating Grad-CAM visualization for model transparency, 
alongside a rule-based engine that converts severity masks 
into actionable crop treatment recommendations.

===== PAGE 7 =====
Lead: Aryaahi Singh (2360342)
Overcoming Traditional Limitations
Standard deep learning models often fail to isolate disease symptoms 
from complex environmental backgrounds. By implementing precise 
spatial localization models like YOLOv8 and U-Net, we accurately 
segment infected regions. This filters out background noise and 
provides the exact pixel ratio required to mathematically estimate 
crop disease severity.
